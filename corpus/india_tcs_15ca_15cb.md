Indian income-tax rules can require tax collection at source (TCS) or Form 15CA/15CB documentation for certain outward remittances. The exact threshold, rate, exemption, and documentation requirement changes by purpose, amount, tax residency, and current notification.

Use this item as a checklist source only. Verify the current rate and form requirement from the Income Tax Department or a qualified CA before initiating or reporting a remittance.
