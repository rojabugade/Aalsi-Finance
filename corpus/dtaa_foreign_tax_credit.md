Double Taxation Avoidance Agreement concepts and foreign tax credit mechanisms can reduce or coordinate taxation across countries, but eligibility depends on residency, income character, sourcing rules, documentation, and current law.

This corpus item should be used to explain concepts and point users to professional review. It must not be used to produce personalized tax filing instructions.
